Privacy Policy

citAI Nexus Backup | Updated 21 November 2026

Purpose

citAI Nexus Backup is a private configuration of the open-source rclone utility, used solely by its account owner to back up selected local geospatial project files to their own Google Drive account.

Data access

The application requests the Google OAuth permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which allows it to create and manage files associated with the application. It does not request unrestricted access to the account's other Google Drive content.

Data processing

  • Backup files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and their own Google Drive account.
  • The website does not collect or receive files, Google user data, OAuth credentials, or access tokens.
  • rclone stores authentication tokens locally on the owner's computer and may keep operational logs (file names, paths, sizes, transfer outcomes, and errors) under the owner's control.
  • Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with third parties by this application.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls the backup files and their retention in Google Drive. Access can be revoked at any time using Google Account settings. Revoking authorization prevents future access but does not automatically remove already uploaded backup files.

Security

The application uses Google OAuth and does not request the account password. Credentials and backup data are not published on this website.

Contact

The contact address for this application is provided on its Google OAuth consent screen.
